Privacy Policy

Effective date: 1 August 2026  ·  Bellows Applied Intelligence Solutions (BAIS)

WorkCalc does not collect, transmit, or share any personal information. Everything the app stores stays on your device.

Who we are

WorkCalc is developed and published by Bellows Applied Intelligence Solutions (BAIS), a software company based in Ontario, Canada. You can reach us at BellowsAIS@outlook.com.

What data WorkCalc collects

WorkCalc collects no personal data. The app does not ask for your name, email address, location, device identifiers, or any other information that could identify you.

There is no account system, no login, no registration, and no analytics or crash-reporting service embedded in the app.

What is stored on your device

WorkCalc saves two small pieces of information in your browser's local storage. This data never leaves your device:

  • Unit preference — whether you last used metric or imperial. Stored so the app remembers your choice between sessions.
  • Calculation history — the last 20 results you copied using the "Copy result" button. Stored so you can refer back to recent calculations.

You can clear this data at any time using the "Clear all" button in the History tab, or by clearing your browser's site data in your device settings.

Third-party services

WorkCalc is available on Android via the Google Play Store. When you download or update the app through Google Play, Google's own privacy practices apply to that interaction. We have no access to any data Google collects as part of the Play Store distribution process. For details, see Google's Privacy Policy.

Beyond the Play Store distribution channel, WorkCalc makes no connections to any third-party service at runtime.

Children's privacy

WorkCalc does not knowingly collect information from children under 13. The app collects no personal information from anyone, regardless of age.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes in a material way, we will update the effective date at the top of this page. Because WorkCalc collects no personal data, changes are unlikely unless the app's features change substantially.
