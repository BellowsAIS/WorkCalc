## Full description

WorkCalc gives you fast, reliable material estimates right on the job site — no internet required.

Built for Canadian tradespeople, WorkCalc covers the calculations you reach for every day. Enter your dimensions, pick metric or imperial, and get a result instantly. No submit button, no account, no ads.

**Six built-in calculators:**

**Concrete** — Slab volume, rectangular footings, and round piers. Bag count for standard Canadian 25 kg and 30 kg bags included.

**Lumber & Framing** — Board feet from nominal dimensions, and stud count for 12", 16", and 24" on-centre spacing. Wall height shown in the result so you can order the right stud length.

**Masonry** — Brick and concrete block count with adjustable mortar joint width. Subtract door and window openings before the calc runs. Mortar volume included.

**Roofing** — Roof squares and shingle bundles from plan area and pitch. Rafter length and ridge length shown in the result.

**Paint & Coatings** — Wall area with door and window deductions. Adjustable spread rate and coat count. Total litres or gallons ready to quote.

**Excavation** — Bank volume and loose volume with swell factor presets for sand, topsoil, clay, and rock. Optional truck load count.

**Key features:**
- Results update live on every keystroke — no submit button needed
- Adjustable waste factor on every calculator (default 10%)
- Plain-language formula shown under every result so you can sense-check the math
- Copy result to clipboard — paste directly into a quote or email
- Calculation history keeps your last 20 results
- Full offline support — works with no signal on site
- Metric and imperial — switch with one tap, persists between sessions
- Dark mode supported

**Disclaimer:** WorkCalc produces estimates only. Always verify quantities before ordering materials. Results are provided for planning purposes and do not replace professional measurement or engineering review.
